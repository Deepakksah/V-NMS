HOW TO INSTALL:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Option 1: Complete Fresh Setup (Recommended)
   1. Right-click on 'V-NMS-Enterprise-Setup.exe'
   2. Select 'Run as administrator'
   3. Click 'Install V-NMS Enterprise Now'
   4. Done! Dashboard will automatically launch in your browser.

Option 2: Standalone MSI Installation (If PostgreSQL is already installed)
   1. Double-click on 'V-NMS.msi'
   2. Follow on-screen wizard prompts to complete installation.
